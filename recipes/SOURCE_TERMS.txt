DATA SOURCES AND ATTRIBUTION
Retrieved 2026-10-22. These datasets were not authored by the application author.
Original recipe texts and source links are retained; images were not downloaded.

1. RuRecipes-93k — Evgenii Pishchik (2025)
   Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/epishchik/RuRecipes-93k
   Original source: https://www.russianfood.com/
   The dataset publisher labels the corpus CC BY-NC 4.0.
   License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/
   Full license is saved as sources/CC-BY-NC-4.0.txt.
   Changes in this distribution: structural conversion, ingredient normalization,
   Russian aliases, search indices and optional/alternative ingredient grouping.
   Source description and attribution are saved in sources/RuRecipes-93k.README.md.
   This is the dataset publisher's license statement, not an independent clearance
   of rights held by individual original recipe authors.

2. RecipeNLG — Bień et al. (2020), Poznań University of Technology
   Authors: Michał Bień, Michał Gilski, Martyna Maciejewska, Wojciech Taisner,
   Dawid Wisniewski, Agnieszka Lawrynowicz.
   Project: https://recipenlg.cs.put.poznan.pl/
   Paper: https://aclanthology.org/2020.inlg-1.4/
   Dataset card with terms: https://huggingface.co/datasets/mbien/recipe_nlg
   Actual data mirror: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Mahimas/recipenlg
   The mirror does not grant a new license. Original RecipeNLG restrictions apply.
   Changes in this distribution: structural conversion, Russian ingredient mapping,
   aliases, search indices and optional/alternative ingredient grouping.
